# Editable estimated bumper materials

Select a preset in Component Designer → Bump curve → Prepare STEP bumper / fixtures → Material. Selection copies three coefficients into the current recipe. Edit those fields and save; reopening preserves the exact values. Preset changes do not rewrite other saved recipes. Invalid raw entries remain recoverable. These presets are Estimated, not calibrated. They do not bypass the unpassed folding/contact backend gate.

TPU85A uses an initial Young modulus of20MPa; TPU95A uses48.4MPa, from the printed XY tensile data in the [85A datasheet](https://ultrafusefff.jp/basf3d/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ultrafuse_TPU_85A_TDS_EN_v2.5.pdf) and [95A datasheet](https://move.forward-am.com/hubfs/AES%20Documentation/Flexible%20Filaments/TPU%2095A/TDS/Ultrafuse_TPU-95A_TDS_EN_v1.0.pdf). TPU90A interpolates34.2MPa. That interpolation is an application assumption, not a hardness-to-modulus law. Poisson ratio0.49, isotropy and neo-Hookean extension from tensile to compression are also assumptions. The editable coefficients are C1=E/[4(1+nu)], C2=0, K=E/[3(1-2nu)], with FEBio pressure_model2. This does not reproduce rate dependence, hysteresis or print-direction behavior. The exploratory maximum compression fraction is0.30, not an experimentally established validity limit.

Microcellular PU350–650kg/m³ uses approximate guide-curve scales from [BASF Cellasto technical information, Fig1](https://www.basf.com/dam/jcr:0af1eb5a-0ae0-3a4b-84e0-7cf7e6f2511d/basf/www/kr/documents/ko/product/Cellasto_technical_brochure.pdf). Initial E estimates are0.50/0.65/0.90/1.20/1.55/1.90/2.30MPa respectively; assumed nu=0.05 represents low lateral expansion. These are engineering starting estimates, not digitized fits or published manufacturer coefficients. A one-term compressible Ogden energy uses cp=E*nu/[(1+nu)(1-2nu)], c1=E/(1+nu), m1=2. The editable fields are cp,c1,m1. Its exploratory range ends at0.50 compression; high-compression densification is not calibrated by this estimate.

All coefficients, exact source/assumption records and conditions are included in the immutable recipe identity. The original local BASF brochure and inspected chart are retained under artifacts/material-research. No infill percentage multiplies stiffness. Explicit CAD internal geometry and a homogenized printed structure remain different model choices. Material-to-configured-car binding is still unfinished; current manual overrides belong to the saved Studio recipe.
