# Pure-longitudinal RSD semantics

RSD is the front share of current tangent roll stiffness. In the linear four-corner model, pure longitudinal acceleration produces pitch and longitudinal load transfer, but does not alter spring, ARB, or bump-stop tangent rates. RSD and RCB must therefore remain invariant.

An RSD change under pure longitudinal acceleration is meaningful only when an explicitly reported tangent input changed at the current operating point: bump-stop engagement, nonlinear spring rate, geometry-derived motion ratio or ARB mapping, or another included nonlinear element. It is not an independent longitudinal load-transfer metric.
